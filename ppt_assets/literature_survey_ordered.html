1 Speech biomarkers of Parkinson's disease: Advances and open challenges Author: Vasquez-Correa et al. 2024 Biomedical Signal Processing and Control Reviewed speech biomarkers such as jitter, shimmer, HNR, DFA, RPDE, and PPE for Parkinson's disease analysis. Clearly explains important speech biomarkers linked with motor dysfunction. Mainly a review study; does not provide a complete implementation model.
2 Remote speech analysis for monitoring Parkinson's disease progression Author: Tsanas et al. 2024 IEEE Reviews in Biomedical Engineering Used remote speech analysis and sustained phonation tasks to monitor Parkinson's disease progression. Supports non-invasive and remote monitoring through speech signals. Performance depends on recording quality and controlled speaking conditions.
3 Speech-based biomarkers for early Parkinson's disease detection: Advances in acoustic analysis and machine learning Author: Xu et al. 2025 IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering Applied acoustic feature extraction with ML models such as SVM, Random Forest, and boosting methods. Achieved strong performance using acoustic biomarkers for early detection. Model reliability depends on dataset size, feature quality, and generalization.
4 Transformer-based speech representation learning for Parkinson's disease detection Author: SedighMalekroodi et al. 2025 Computer Speech & Language Used transformer-based speech representation learning for Parkinson's disease classification. Learns complex temporal and spectral speech patterns automatically. Requires large datasets and high computational resources.
5 Quantum machine learning for clinical decision support systems: Opportunities and limitations Author: Chow 2025 Nature Digital Medicine Reviewed quantum machine learning for clinical decision support systems. Highlights future potential of QML in healthcare applications. Current quantum systems have qubit limitations and noise-related issues.
