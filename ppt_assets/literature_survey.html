1 Machine Learning-Based Classification of Parkinson's Disease Patients Using Speech Biomarkers Author: Hossain & Amenta 2024 Journal of Parkinson's Disease Used supervised ML pipelines on speech biomarkers with 756 samples and 754 acoustic features. Shows that ML pipelines can classify PD using speech biomarkers. Uses high-dimensional features and limited model diversity; no quantum or swarm model comparison.
2 A Study on Voice Measures in Patients with Parkinson's Disease Author: Xiu et al. 2024 Journal of Voice Analyzed jitter, shimmer, HNR, formants, and trained SVM classifiers using selected voice measures. Uses clinically understandable acoustic voice measures for low-cost screening. Small dataset of 30 PD and 30 healthy speakers; limited model comparison.
3 Machine Learning-Assisted Speech Analysis for Early Detection of Parkinson's Disease Author: Di Cesare et al. 2024 Sensors Studied speech analysis using speaker diarization and classification techniques for early PD detection. Highlights speech as a non-invasive and accessible biomarker. Performance depends on recording quality, noise conditions, and sufficient data collection.
4 Machine Learning-Based Classification Using Acoustic Features: Insights from Multilingual Speech Tasks Author: Jeong et al. 2024 Computers in Biology and Medicine Used eGeMAPSv2 acoustic features and a voting-based ML model on Korean PD speech data. Achieved 84.73% accuracy and 92.18% AUC with limited training data. Focused mainly on one speech cohort; no quantum, swarm, or interactive explainability dashboard.
5 Transformer-Based Transfer Learning on Self-Reported Voice Recordings for Parkinson's Diagnosis Author: Tougui et al. 2024 Scientific Reports Used CNN and Transformer-based transfer learning on smartphone self-reported voice recordings. Strong deep learning performance using transfer learning on real-world voice data. Requires large data and high computation; interpretability is lower than structured SHAP explanations.
